FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Non-IT Consulting?

Non-IT consulting focuses on the business functions that support an organization’s overall performance, including finance, HR, operations, supply chain, project management, compliance, and business transformation.

What types of non-IT professionals does DataServ provide?

DataServ can support organizations with professionals across finance and accounting, HR, operations, supply chain, project and program management, risk and compliance, business analysis, and change management.

Can DataServ support both consulting and staffing requirements?

Yes. Engagements can be structured around consulting, contract staffing, contract-to-hire, direct hire, staff augmentation, or project-based teams depending on the organization’s needs.

Can you support short-term projects?

Yes. Organizations can engage professionals for defined projects, temporary workforce requirements, transformation initiatives, or longer-term operational needs.

Do you provide professionals across multiple industries?

Yes. DataServ supports business functions across industries including healthcare, financial services, retail and e-commerce, manufacturing, technology, and other business environments.

How do I get started?

Tell us about your business requirement, the function you need support with, and the type of expertise you’re looking for. Our team can help identify an appropriate engagement model and professional capability.
