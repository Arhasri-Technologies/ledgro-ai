01 / WHO WE ARE

A company built around the work and the people.

Dataserv was started by technologists with long experience in e-business, enterprise integration, business intelligence, and ERP, and in running an open-ended software training and development institute.

That mix is still how the company is organized. Software development, global outsourcing, and professional staffing sit in one place, so the system and the people who run it are hired and built together.

The aim is to stay a reliable source of skilled software engineers, and to be the outsourcing partner a client can hold to a deadline. We keep adapting as the tools change, and we still measure the work by whether it meets the expectation on time and at a cost the brief can justify.

DATASERV / 01
IT STAFFING

Capacity, when the team needs it.

We place skilled IT professionals with clients who need people to meet a business demand and keep growing. Engagements cover staff augmentation, contract work, and specialists in software, data, cloud, and AI.

  • Engineers who join the team, not a résumé left at the door
  • Technical leadership when a program needs a senior hand
  • People who can work inside the client’s stack and standards
DATASERV / 02
GLOBAL OUTSOURCING

Design and software, delivered with you.

Outsourcing covers logo design, web design, and application development for clients across regions. The same standard applies: a clear brief, a working result, and a team that stays through delivery.

  • Identity, web, and product design that belong to the same company
  • Application development from a signed scope to a running system
  • Delivery that does not disappear after the first release

02 / OUR PEOPLE

The human element holds the system.

We recruit carefully, train people in the technologies the work actually uses, and give them room to operate across business domains.

DataServ treats that human element as the center of a field that changes quickly. Tools move. The person accountable for the outcome does not. Competitive edge, in this company, is the people who can still make a sound decision when the stack is new.

  1. 01

    Recruit

    Look for people who can do the work, then put them on problems that are actually hard.

  2. 02

    Train

    Develop them across technologies and industries, so nobody is locked to one stack.

  3. 03

    Empower

    Give them the decision, and keep a person — not a tool — responsible for the result.

03 / CORE VALUES

The principles behind the delivery.

Four values guide the work. They are how a system gets designed, not a poster in the office.

01

Engineering excellence

Quality is part of the build: architecture someone else can maintain, a review before anything is called done, and practice that holds up after the demo.

Sound structure · review before release

02

Security & compliance

Access, data handling, and the controls an enterprise expects are decided while the system is designed. Security is not a checklist applied after launch.

Access · data care · controls from the start

03

Responsible AI

When a model is in the product, a person stays accountable. The system should be understandable to the team that owns it, and limited to the job it was given.

Understandable · limited · a person accountable

04

Client success

We stay past the kickoff. A partnership is the system running in the business, the people who can operate it, and the next change when the work shifts.

After launch · shared outcome

01 / DISCOVER

Start with the right questions.

We map the business need, the people using the system, and what success should look like. A shared brief gives the whole team a clear direction.

  1. 01

    Discover

    The business need, the users, the systems already in place, and what “done” means.

  2. 02

    Design

    Architecture, experience, integrations, and the data the product actually depends on.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Short iterations, a working increment, and a review before the next one starts.

  4. 04

    Validate

    Behavior, performance, and security checked against the brief — not against a hope.

  5. 05

    Launch

    Ship it, support it, and keep improving it once people are using it.
