03 / FROM KNOWLEDGE TO LAUNCH

A clear process.
At every step.

Each stage ends with something you can review. Decisions, ownership and acceptance criteria stay visible throughout.

  1. 01

    Discover & transfer knowledge

    Map users, business rules, existing systems and the problem to solve.

    DELIVERABLE / A shared brief and prioritized scope
  2. 02

    Design the flow

    Create journey maps, wireframes and an interactive prototype. Check accessibility and error states.

    DELIVERABLE / An approved experience and design system
  3. 03

    Plan the architecture

    Choose interfaces, APIs, data models, permissions and hosting around the expected workload.

    DELIVERABLE / A technical plan and delivery milestones
  4. 04

    Build with AI, review with people

    Use AI for implementation support, tests and repetitive work. Engineers review changes and integrate working increments.

    DELIVERABLE / Reviewable builds and tracked decisions
  5. 05

    Validate under real conditions

    Test critical journeys, security boundaries, accessibility and representative load. Agree release criteria with your team.

    DELIVERABLE / Evidence against the acceptance criteria
  6. 06

    Launch, observe & improve

    Release with monitoring and rollback ready. Transfer knowledge, measure usage and prioritize the next improvement.

    DELIVERABLE / A supported application and improvement backlog
