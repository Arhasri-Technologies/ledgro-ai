FAQ

Questions we hear before a roadmap.

What does an IT consulting engagement cover?

Cloud, integration, ERP, and architecture, from discovery through rollout. Security, data flows, and AI readiness are assessed before budget is committed.

Do you only write the plan?

A roadmap can stand on its own. When you want the same team through delivery, the engagement continues into rollout and a handoff your people can run.

How does this relate to app development?

App development builds the product. IT consulting is the architecture, integration, and operating plan around it. They can run together or separately.

How do we start?

Tell us where systems don’t connect and what a rollout would have to survive. We’ll scope discovery before a budget conversation.
