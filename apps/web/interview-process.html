Our Process

What to expect.

2–3 weeks, start to offer

  1. 01 3–5 business days

    Application Review

    Our team reviews your resume, portfolio, and cover letter. We look for relevant experience, projects, and cultural alignment.

  2. 02 30 minutes

    Recruiter Call

    A friendly chat to learn about your background, career goals, and answer any questions about DataServ and the role.

  3. 03 2–4 hours

    Technical Assessment

    A take-home assignment or live coding session focused on real-world problems. We care about your approach, not just the answer.

  4. 04 60 minutes · senior roles

    System Design

    A discussion of architecture, trade-offs, and how you approach complex technical challenges.

  5. 05 45 minutes

    Culture & Values

    Meet team members and leadership to discuss collaboration, communication, and how you approach work challenges.

  6. 06 1–2 business days

    Offer & Onboarding

    We extend an offer and begin your personalized onboarding journey to set you up for success.

Pro Tips

How to succeed.

  • 01

    Research us

    Understand our services, clients, and recent projects. Show genuine interest in what we do.

  • 02

    Showcase projects

    Prepare to discuss your best work. What challenges did you face? What did you learn?

  • 03

    Ask questions

    Great questions show engagement. Ask about team culture, projects, and growth opportunities.

  • 04

    Think aloud

    In technical interviews, share your thought process. We value how you approach problems.

  • 05

    Be authentic

    It's okay not to know everything. Honesty about gaps shows self-awareness and integrity.

  • 06

    Test your setup

    Check your camera, mic, and internet before remote interviews. First impressions matter.

What We Look For

Skills that matter.

Technical

  • Problem-solving ability
  • Clean code principles
  • System design thinking
  • Learning velocity

AI & Automation

  • LLM/GPT understanding
  • API integration skills
  • Data pipeline knowledge
  • ML fundamentals

Soft skills

  • Clear communication
  • Collaboration mindset
  • Ownership & accountability
  • Adaptability

Mindset

  • Growth orientation
  • Curiosity & initiative
  • Resilience
  • Customer focus

Remote Interviews

From your home office.

  • 01 Test your camera, microphone, and internet beforehand
  • 02 Choose a quiet, well-lit space with a neutral background
  • 03 Have a glass of water and a notepad ready
  • 04 Dress professionally — business casual is perfect
  • 05 Close extra apps and silence notifications
  • 06 Look at the camera when you speak
